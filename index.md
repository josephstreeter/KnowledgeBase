# Madison College Technology Services

## Identity and Access Management Team

The Madison College Technology Services Identity and Access Management (IAM) Team is dedicated to ensuring secure, reliable, and user-friendly access to college technology resources for students, faculty, and staff.

**Our Mission:**

- Protect the college’s digital assets and sensitive information
- Enable seamless access to systems and applications
- Support compliance with security and privacy regulations
- Provide expert guidance and support for authentication, authorization, and account management

**What We Do:**

- Manage user accounts, passwords, and multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Administer access to Microsoft 365, Exchange Online, Active Directory, and other college systems
- Assist with troubleshooting sign-in issues and account recovery
- Implement security best practices and identity governance
- Educate the campus community on safe technology use

**Contact Us:**

- For IAM support, visit the [Madison College Technology Services Portal](https://madisoncollege.edu/technology-services) or contact the Help Desk.
