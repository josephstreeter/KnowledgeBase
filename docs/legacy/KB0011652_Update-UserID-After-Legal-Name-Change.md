## Description

When a legal name change is performed for a student or employee, the user has the option of updating their UserID to reflect the new legal name. This only applies to legal name changes and will not be performed for preferred name changes.

This document outlines the steps the name change requestor must follow in order to complete a name change request.

**NOTE:**

_UserID is also used as the alias portion of your email address (i.e. the portion of the email address to the left of the “@”). Because the UserID and email alias are always identical, for the remainder of this document the use of UserID includes email alias as well._

## Details

Once the legal name change process has been completed, the requestor will have the option of updating their UserID.

**Students:** Contact the Help Desk to request that the UserID be updated following a completed legal name change. An incident will be created and assigned to the Identity and Access Management (IAM) Team.

**Employees:** During the legal name change process in Workday you will be asked if you would like your UserID updated. Confirming that you would like to have your UserID updated will result in HR submitting an incident to the Identity and Access Management Team on your behalf.

## Information

The IAM Team will initiate a process that will begin with sending an approval email to both your Madison College email address and personal email address (if listed in Student System). This approval email will display your current UserID and the new UserID that has been generated.

To approve the name change, click the "Approve" button at the bottom of the email to initiate the change and your account will be updated with the new UserID.

        ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=844922fe1b17d2909446975b234bcba4)

If you change your mind and no longer wish to perform the name change, click the "Reject" button.

**NOTE:**

_The only way to initiate the name change is to click the “approve” button in the email. The email is sent from a mailbox that is not monitored, replies to the approval email will not initiate the process and are not likely to be seen.

Additional information about UserID changes:

    * Your new account name is system generated and cannot be changed.
    * ALL your existing mailbox information from your old email address will be retained under your new email address.
    * Your existing password will remain the same.
    * Your new account name may take up to 24 hours to display correctly in your offline Global Address Book (Outlook Desktop Client).
    * Any devices you are signed into with your old UserID will need to be re-configured using your new UserID.

There may be some delays for some systems (e.g. Email, Blackboard, MyMadisonCollege, and Workday, etc.) as the new UserID is synchronized across all of our systems.
