## Description

Users that have full access to a mailbox or have Owner permissions to a mailbox can provide calendar permissions for other users. Owners can provide either Editor or Reviewer permissions for a user.

## Details

The following information applies to the Office 2016 Outlook desktop client.

## Information

1. In Outlook, click the Calendar icon from the bottom-left corner of the window.

        ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=f6c44e991bb35a10417dcaa1604bcb70)

2. Right-click on one of the calendars and select **Properties**.

3. Click the **Permissions** tab.

4. Click **Add...** to grant a user(s) permission or highlight an existing user and modify their permissions.
