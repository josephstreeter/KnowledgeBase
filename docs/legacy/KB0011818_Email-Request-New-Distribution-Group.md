## Description

Sometimes called Distribution Lists, are groups that can be used to distribute messages. Distribution Groups may have owners assigned to manage the members or they may be dynamic, meaning the members are populated based on data (e.g. employee type, cost center, location, etc.).

## Details

A new Distribution Group may be requested by submitting an Incident in ServiceNow with all of the required information.

_
_

* *
**## Information

    * Alias – This is the text that appears to the left of the ‘@’ in the email address. All aliases will begin with “MC-DL-“ and then include custom text provided by the customer.
    * Display Name
    * List of Owners (UserID) – Owners are capable of managing the group’s membership
    * List of users who can “Send As” the group (UserID) – These users can send a message as the group. This means that when the message is delivered, it will show the group name in the “from” field and not the individual who sent the message.
    * Hide from Global Address List (Yes/No) – This setting determines if the group is displayed in the Global Address List (GAL) or is hidden.
    * Allowed Senders (UserID) – This is a list of users who are permitted to send messages to the group. If no Allowed Senders are configured anyone may send messages to the group.

## Additional Information and Follow Up

Contact the Help Desk at extension 6666 if you have any questions about the information listed above.
