## Description

A Distribution Group is a group of recipients. Sending an email to a Distribution Group is a great way to deliver a message to a group of people simultaneously. Some Distribution Groups are populated using business data (e.g. all students or all staff in a department), but others are managed in an ad-hoc fashion by assigning owners to them. Being assigned as an owner of a Distribution Group allows that individual to manually manage the membership as needed.

## Details

This document demonstrates how to add or delete members from a distribution list using the Outlook Desktop Client. You must be listed as the owner of the distribution group to perform this task.

## Information

  1. Open the Outlook Desktop application installed on your computer
  2. Click on the Home tab and select the Address Book
  3. In the Address Book dialog box, select **All Distribution Lists** under **Address Book:**

      ![Address Book Distribution List](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=7cfa22721b57d2909446975b234bcb11)

  4. In the Search box enter the name of your distribution list
  5. Double-click your distribution list
  6. To Add a member:

     1. Click the **Modify Members** button
     2. Click Add
     3. In the **Add Users** dialog box, search for the member you wish to add
     4. Double-click their name and then click **OK**.
  7. To Delete a member

     1. Click the **Modify Members** button
     2. In the **Contact Group Membership** dialog box, highlight the user you wish to remove
     3. Click **Delete**
     4. Click OK:
