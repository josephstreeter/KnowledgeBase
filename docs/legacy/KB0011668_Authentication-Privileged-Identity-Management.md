## Description

Some administrative privileges are being managed through Privileged Identity Management (PIM). This allows for administrators to only elevate their privileges when needed.

## Details

The following instructions outline how to use PIM to elevate a particular role for an administrator.

## Information

To elevate privileges:

  1. Log into the Azure Portal (**[https://portal.azure.com](https://portal.azure.com/ "<https://portal.azure.com>")**)
  2. Type "**Privileged Identity Management** " in the search bar at the top
  3. Click "Privileged Identity Management" and click "**My Roles** " in the left pane
  4. Click "**Activate** " next to the role to be elevated
  5. The Activate flyout will appear on the right, click the "**Activate** " button at the bottom
  6. Provide MFA if required
  7. Wait for stages 1-3 to complete, page will refresh
  8. Complete

## Additional Information and Follow Up

If you have any issues using PIM to elevate permissions, contact the Identity and Access Management Team.
