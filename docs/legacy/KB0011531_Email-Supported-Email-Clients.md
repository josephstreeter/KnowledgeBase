## Description

Madison College Microsoft 365 Exchange Online accounts can be configured on a wide variety of email clients.?However, due to the complexity introduced by using non-Microsoft email clients, only a few clients are supported by Technology Services.

## Details

## Information

Supported email clients

* Outlook Web App browsers
  * [Microsoft Edge](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/edge)
  * [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/new/)
  * [Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/?brand=FKPE&geo=US&gclid=EAIaIQobChMIlenDrZmi9AIVVm1vBB2a7wsvEAAYASAAEgIJ_fD_BwE&gclsrc=aw.ds)
  * [Safari](https://www.apple.com/safari/)
* Office 365 ProPlus or Outlook 2019 (Windows or Mac)
* Outlook Mobile App (Android or IOS)

Unsupported email clients

The TS will provide "best effort" support for the following clients, but issues that are not resolved by basic troubleshooting will likely not be solved.

* Apple Mail and iCalendar for desktop and mobile clients
* Mozilla Thunderbird
* Native mobile apps (i.e. Android native or 3rd party email apps for Android and iOS)
* Any other email clients not listed under Supported email client's section above

Outlook Web App (aka OWA)

OWA requires only a supported browser on your device. To access Madison College Exchange Online using Outlook Web App (OWA):

  1. Open the supported browser of your choice and navigate to<<https://outlook.com/madisoncollege.edu>>
  2. Login using your Madison College user ID and password. If you are prompted to set the time zone, select it from the dropdown list.
  3. To log out of OWA, in the top right corner of the page, click your profile photo (avatar), and then click?Sign out.

Office 365 ProPlus or Outlook 2019

Outlook 2019 should already be installed as an application on your desktop or laptop. If you do not see this application, please contact the Help Desk to have a version of the Outlook desktop app installed.

Outlook Mobile App

The supported Outlook mobile app and instructions can be found [here](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/outlook-for-ios-and-android-help-cd84214e-a5ac-4e95-9ea3-e07f78d0cde6?redirectsourcepath=%252fen-us%252flearn%252foutlook-for-ios-and-android-faq&ui=en-us&rs=en-us&ad=us).
