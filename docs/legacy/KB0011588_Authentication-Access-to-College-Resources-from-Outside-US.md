## Description

This document describes controls that have been put in place to restrict remote access to college resources from some countries outside the US.

## Details

The requirements for accessing college resources from outside the US are the same as those for accessing them from off-campus locations within the US. However, there is a list of countries, referred to as "Threat Countries," which, due to their high level of cyber-threat activity, are restricted by policy from accessing most college services.

A student attempting to access college resources from a Threat Country will receive an error message similar to the one below.

## Information

Campus Solutions remains accessible from these countries, but all other resources require the use of the Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI) service.

Students outside the US but not in a Threat Country need only to complete multifactor authentication to access college services directly on a personal mobile device or computer.

## Exemptions

Students located in a Threat Country can be exempted from this policy by submitting their Name/Student ID to the Student Help Desk.

Employees located in Threat Countries will not be granted an exemption and will have to use the Virtual Private Network (VPN) or Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI) services to access college resources.

## Additional Information and Follow Up

If you are a student and need to request an exemption, please contact the Student Help Desk with your Name and Student ID.
