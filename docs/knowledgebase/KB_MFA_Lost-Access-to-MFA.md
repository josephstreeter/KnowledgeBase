## Description

Guidance for users who have lost access to all registered MFA methods.

## Details

If you lose access to all registered MFA methods (Authenticator app, FIDO2 key, phone number):

1. Try to sign in and select "I can't use any of my authentication methods" if available.
2. Contact your IT support or help desk for identity verification and account recovery.
3. Be prepared to provide proof of identity (employee ID, alternate contact, etc.).
4. IT may issue a Temporary Access Pass (TAP) or reset your MFA settings.

**Tips:**

- Register multiple authentication methods to avoid lockout.
- Keep your contact information up to date.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If you cannot access any method, escalate to IT immediately and request a TAP or manual reset.
- If you regain access to one method, use it to add backup methods in your security info.
- For urgent cases, request escalation to the IAM team and document your recovery steps for future reference.

**References:**

- [Microsoft: Recover your account](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/user-help/user-help-account-recovery)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

For urgent issues, escalate to IAM team or follow your organization's recovery process.
