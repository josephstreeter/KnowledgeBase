## Description

Instructions for managing your authentication methods in the My Sign-Ins portal.

## Details

To manage your authentication methods in the My Sign-Ins portal:

1. Go to [My Sign-Ins Security Info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info).
2. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 or Entra ID account.
3. View your registered methods (Authenticator App, FIDO2 key, phone, etc.).
4. Add, remove, or change authentication methods as needed.
5. Follow prompts to verify changes.

**Tips:**

- Keep multiple methods registered for backup.
- Remove outdated or unused methods.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If unable to add or remove a method, check for browser compatibility and update your browser.
- If changes do not save, clear browser cache and cookies, then retry.
- For persistent issues, contact IT support for manual updates.

**References:**

- [Manage authentication methods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/user-help/user-help-security-info-manage-methods)

Contact IT support for help managing your authentication methods.
