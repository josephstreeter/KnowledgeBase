## Description

Steps to switch from password-based to passwordless sign-in.

## Details

To switch from password-based to passwordless sign-in:

1. Go to [My Sign-Ins Security Info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info).
2. Sign in and select "Add method".
3. Choose a passwordless option (Authenticator App, FIDO2 key, TAP).
4. Follow prompts to complete setup.
5. Remove password-based methods if desired.
6. Test your passwordless sign-in.

**Tips:**

- Register multiple passwordless methods for backup.
- Contact IT for help with migration.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If passwordless options are not available, contact IT to confirm eligibility and policy settings.
- If setup fails, update your browser and clear cache/cookies.
- For FIDO2 keys, check for firmware updates and use a supported browser.
- For Authenticator App, ensure notifications are enabled and app is updated.

**References:**

- [Switch to passwordless](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/authentication/concept-authentication-passwordless)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

Contact IT support for help switching authentication methods.
