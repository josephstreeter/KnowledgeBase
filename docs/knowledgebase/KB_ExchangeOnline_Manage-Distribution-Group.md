## Description

Instructions for managing distribution group membership in Exchange Online.

## Details

Distribution groups are email lists that allow you to send messages to multiple people at once using a single email address. For example, sending an email to "<marketing-team@madisoncollege.edu>" will deliver the message to all members of that group. You can join or leave certain distribution groups yourself, while others require approval from a group owner or administrator. Understanding how to manage your group memberships helps ensure you receive relevant communications for your role or interests.

## Information

### Prerequisites

- You must have an active Madison College email account
- You must be signed in to Outlook Web App or Outlook desktop client
- Some groups may require approval from the group owner

### How to Find Available Distribution Groups

#### Method 1: Through Outlook Web App

1. Sign in to [Outlook Web App](https://outlook.office.com)
2. Click the "People" icon in the left navigation
3. Click "Directory" at the top of the People page
4. Use the search box to find groups by name or department
5. Look for entries marked as "Distribution List" or "Group"

#### Method 2: Through Global Address List

1. When composing a new email, click "To:"
2. In the address book, change the dropdown from "Contacts" to "Global Address List"
3. Browse or search for distribution groups

### How to Join or Leave a Distribution Group (Self-Service)

**Important:** Not all groups allow self-service joining. If you don't see join/leave options, contact the group owner or IT support.

#### Via Outlook Web App

1. Sign in to [Outlook Web App](https://outlook.office.com)
2. Click the "Settings" gear icon in the top-right corner
3. Click "View all Outlook settings" at the bottom of the settings panel
4. Navigate to "Mail" > "Automatic replies"
5. On the left menu, click "Groups"
6. Find the distribution group you want to join or leave
7. Click "Join group" or "Leave group" button

#### Via Outlook Desktop Client

1. Open Outlook desktop application
2. Go to "File" > "Account Settings" > "Account Settings"
3. Click the "Address Books" tab
4. Select "Global Address List" and click "Change"
5. Find the distribution group and right-click
6. Select "Add to group" or "Remove from group" if available

### How to Manage Group Membership (For Group Owners Only)

If you are a group owner, you can manage membership through:

#### Via Exchange Admin Center

- Contact IT support for access to the Exchange Admin Center
- Navigate to "Recipients" > "Groups"
- Select your group and manage members

#### Via Outlook Web App (Owners)

- Some owner functions may be available through the Groups section in Outlook Web App settings

## Additional Information and Follow-up

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

#### I can't find the group I'm looking for

- Try searching with different keywords or abbreviations
- Ask the group owner for the exact group name
- Contact IT support if the group should exist but isn't visible

#### Join/Leave buttons are not available

- The group may require approval - contact the group owner
- You may not have permission to self-manage membership
- Contact IT support for assistance

#### I joined a group but I'm not receiving emails

- Changes can take up to 30 minutes to sync
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify the group is active by contacting the group owner

### Important Notes

- Changes to group membership may take a few minutes to sync across all services
- Some groups require approval from the group owner to join or leave
- If you cannot manage your group membership yourself, contact the group owner or IT support
- Mobile apps may have limited distribution group management features

### Contact Information

For further assistance with distribution group management:

- Contact Madison College IT Support
- Reach out to the specific group owner (if known)
- Submit a help desk ticket through the college's IT portal
