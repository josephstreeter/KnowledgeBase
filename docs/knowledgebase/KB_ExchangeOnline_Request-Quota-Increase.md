## Description

Instructions for requesting a mailbox quota increase in Exchange Online.

## Details

Mailbox quota increases may be necessary for users with large volumes of email or specific business needs. Requests are subject to organizational policy and Microsoft 365 licensing. Before requesting an increase, clean up your mailbox and archive old items to ensure efficient use of storage. IT support will review your request and advise on next steps.

## Information

### How to Request a Mailbox Quota Increase

1. Contact your IT support or helpdesk and provide your mailbox details.
2. Explain why you need a quota increase (e.g., business need, large volume of email).
3. Your IT team will review your request and may adjust your quota based on licensing and policy.

### What to Expect

- Quota increases are subject to organizational policy and Microsoft 365 licensing.
- You may be asked to clean up your mailbox before a quota increase is approved.
- Changes may take up to 24 hours to apply.

## Additional Information and Follow-up

- Standard mailbox quotas are 50 GB (E1/E3) and 100 GB (E5).
- Archiving and cleaning up your mailbox may resolve quota issues without an increase.

If your request is denied or you need further assistance, contact your IT support or helpdesk.
