## Description

Instructions for updating or changing your registered phone number for MFA.

## Details

To update or change your registered phone number for MFA:

1. Go to [My Sign-Ins Security Info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info).
2. Sign in and locate your registered phone number.
3. Click "Change" or "Remove" next to your number.
4. Enter your new mobile number and verify with a code.
5. Save changes and test sign-in.

**Tips:**

- Keep your phone number up to date to avoid lockout.
- Remove old or unused numbers.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If unable to update your number, clear browser cache and cookies, then retry.
- If verification code is not received, check your phone signal and verify your number is correct.
- For persistent issues, contact IT support for manual update.

**References:**

- [Update phone number for MFA](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/user-help/user-help-security-info-change-phone-number)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

Contact IT support if you have trouble updating your phone number.
