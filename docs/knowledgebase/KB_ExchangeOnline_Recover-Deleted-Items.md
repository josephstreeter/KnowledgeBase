## Description

Instructions for recovering deleted emails and items in Outlook and OWA.

## Details

Exchange Online retains deleted emails and items for a limited time, allowing users to recover them if deleted accidentally. Items in the 'Deleted Items' folder are typically retained for 30 days, and some organizations enable additional recovery from the server for up to 14 days. Recovery options may vary depending on your organization's retention policies and permissions. Always check both the 'Deleted Items' folder and server recovery options before contacting IT support.

## Information

### Recover Deleted Items in Outlook (Windows/Mac)

1. Open Outlook and go to the 'Deleted Items' folder.
2. Locate the email or item you want to recover.
3. Right-click the item and select 'Move' > 'Inbox' or another folder.
4. If the item is not in 'Deleted Items', go to 'Home' > 'Recover Deleted Items from Server'.
5. Select the items to restore and click 'Restore Selected Items'.

### Recover Deleted Items in Outlook Web App (OWA)

1. Sign in to Outlook Web App at [Outlook Web App](https://outlook.office.com).
2. Go to the 'Deleted Items' folder.
3. Select the item and click 'Restore'.
4. If not found, click 'Recover items deleted from this folder' at the top.
5. Select items and click 'Restore'.

## Additional Information and Follow-up

- Items in 'Deleted Items' are retained for 30 days by default.
- After 30 days, items may be recoverable from the server for up to 14 days (if enabled by your organization).
- Permanently deleted items cannot be recovered after retention period expires.

If you cannot recover your item, contact your IT support or helpdesk for further assistance.
