## Description

Steps to recover access if your Microsoft Authenticator device is lost or unavailable.

## Details

Steps to recover access if your Microsoft Authenticator device is lost or unavailable:

1. Try to sign in using an alternate registered method (SMS, FIDO2 key).
2. If no backup method is available, contact IT support for identity verification.
3. IT may issue a Temporary Access Pass (TAP) or reset your authentication methods.
4. Register your new device with the Authenticator App.

**Tips:**

- Always register multiple authentication methods.
- Keep backup codes or alternate contact info.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If unable to recover access, escalate to IT and request a TAP or manual reset.
- If you regain access, immediately add backup authentication methods.
- For persistent issues, document your recovery steps for future reference.

**References:**

- [Recover Authenticator access](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/user-help/user-help-authenticator-app-recovery)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

Contact IT support for urgent recovery or device replacement.
