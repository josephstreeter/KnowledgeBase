## Description

Instructions for updating your contact information in Exchange Online.

## Details

Updating your contact information ensures colleagues and automated systems have accurate details for communication and collaboration. You may need to update your display name, phone number, office location, or job title if you change roles or locations. Some fields may be restricted by your organization and require IT assistance. Always verify changes after updating, as it may take time for updates to sync across Microsoft 365 services.

## Information

### Update Contact Information in Outlook (Windows/Mac)

1. Open Outlook and go to File > Account Settings > Account Settings.
2. Select your account and click 'Change'.
3. Update your contact details (name, email, phone, etc.) as needed.
4. Click 'Next' and then 'Finish'.
5. Restart Outlook to apply changes.

### Update Contact Information in Outlook Web App (OWA)

1. Sign in to Outlook Web App at [Outlook Web App](https://outlook.office.com).
2. Click your profile picture in the top right and select 'View account'.
3. Under 'Personal info', click 'Edit' next to the field you want to update.
4. Make changes and click 'Save'.

### Information That Can Be Updated

- Display Name
- Phone Number
- Office Location
- Job Title
- Email Address (if permitted)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

- Some fields may be managed by your organization and require IT assistance to update.
- Changes may take up to 24 hours to sync across Microsoft 365 services.
- If you cannot update your information, contact your IT support or helpdesk.

For further assistance, contact the Identity and Access Management Team or your local IT support.
