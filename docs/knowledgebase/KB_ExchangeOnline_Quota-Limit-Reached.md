## Description

Guidance for users who reach their mailbox quota or send limit in Exchange Online.

## Details

Mailbox quotas and send limits are enforced to maintain service reliability and prevent abuse. If you reach these limits, you may be unable to send or receive emails until you reduce mailbox size or wait for limits to reset. Regular mailbox maintenance and awareness of limits can help prevent disruptions. If issues persist, IT support can assist with quota increases or troubleshooting.

## Information

### What Happens When You Reach Your Quota or Send Limit

- You may be unable to send or receive new emails.
- You may receive a warning message or a non-delivery report (NDR).
- Your mailbox may stop syncing with Outlook or mobile devices.

### Steps to Take

1. Delete unnecessary emails, especially those with large attachments.
2. Empty your 'Deleted Items' and 'Junk Email' folders.
3. Archive old emails using Outlook's Archive feature.
4. Use OneDrive or SharePoint for sharing large files.
5. If you need to send to more recipients, split your message into smaller groups.

## Additional Information and Follow-up

- Mailbox quotas and send limits are set by your organization and Microsoft 365 licensing.
- If you continue to have issues, you may need to request a quota increase (see related KB article).

If you cannot resolve the issue, contact your IT support or helpdesk for assistance.
