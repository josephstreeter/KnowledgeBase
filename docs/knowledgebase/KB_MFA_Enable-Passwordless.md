## Description

Instructions for enabling passwordless sign-in in your account.

## Details

To enable passwordless sign-in for your account:

1. Go to [My Sign-Ins Security Info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info).
2. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 or Entra ID account.
3. Select "Add method" and choose your preferred passwordless option:
   - Microsoft Authenticator App
   - FIDO2 Security Key
   - Temporary Access Pass (if available)
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete setup.
5. Test your passwordless sign-in by logging out and signing in again.

**Tips:**

- Register more than one passwordless method for backup.
- Keep your devices and keys secure.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If setup fails, check for browser compatibility and update your browser.
- If you do not see passwordless options, contact IT to confirm eligibility.
- For FIDO2 keys, ensure the key is supported and firmware is up to date.
- For Authenticator App, verify notifications are enabled and app is updated.

**References:**

- [Enable passwordless sign-in](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/authentication/howto-authentication-passwordless)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

Contact IT support if you encounter issues during setup.
