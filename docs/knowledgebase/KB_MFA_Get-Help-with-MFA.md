## Description

How to get help or escalate issues related to multi-factor authentication.

## Details

How to get help or escalate issues related to multi-factor authentication (MFA):

1. Review related KB articles for troubleshooting steps.
2. If you cannot resolve the issue, contact your IT support or help desk.
3. Provide details about your issue (error messages, device type, authentication method).
4. For urgent or unresolved issues, request escalation to the IAM team.

**Tips:**

- Keep your contact information up to date.
- Register multiple authentication methods for backup.

**Issue Resolution:**

- If IT support cannot resolve your issue, request escalation and document all troubleshooting steps taken.
- For persistent lockout or technical errors, request a Temporary Access Pass (TAP) or manual reset.
- If you regain access, immediately add backup authentication methods.

**References:**

- [Microsoft: Get help with MFA](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/user-help/user-help-security-info-get-help)

## Additional Information and Follow-up

For persistent issues, escalate to IAM team or follow your organization's support process.
