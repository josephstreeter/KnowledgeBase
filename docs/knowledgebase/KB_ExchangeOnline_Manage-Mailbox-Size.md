## Description

Instructions for managing mailbox size and cleaning up old emails in Outlook.

## Details

Mailbox size management is essential to avoid reaching storage limits and ensure optimal performance in Outlook and Exchange Online. Regularly cleaning up old emails, archiving important messages, and removing large attachments can help keep your mailbox within quota. Organizations may enforce mailbox quotas based on licensing, and exceeding these quotas can prevent you from sending or receiving new emails.

## Information

### How to Check Mailbox Size in Outlook

1. Open Outlook and click 'File'.
2. Select 'Tools' > 'Mailbox Cleanup'.
3. Click 'View Mailbox Size' to see details.

### How to Clean Up Old Emails

- Use 'Mailbox Cleanup' to find items older than a certain date.
- Empty the 'Deleted Items' and 'Junk Email' folders.
- Archive old emails using Outlook's Archive feature.
- Delete large attachments or move them to OneDrive/SharePoint.

### In Outlook Web App (OWA)

1. Sign in to [Outlook Web App](https://outlook.office.com).
2. Right-click folders and select 'Empty folder' to delete all items.
3. Use search to find and delete old or large emails.

## Additional Information and Follow-up

- Mailbox quotas are enforced by Exchange Online; exceeding quota may prevent sending/receiving mail.
- Archiving helps keep mailbox size within limits and improves performance.
- Use OneDrive or SharePoint for storing large files instead of email attachments.

If you need help managing mailbox size or archiving, contact your IT support or helpdesk.
